As technology spreads and opportunities to experiment and build are commoditised, it seems to me that taste will provide freshness and differentiation.

## What taste is...

* A unique angle to a specific problem
* Created with passion and a particular user in mind
* The way it is solved, and feels natural to the user
* Not made for everyone, but usable by everyone
* With quirks that give it a personality

## What taste is not...

* Something generic, focused on performance, productivity or a low-cost offer
* Expansion built on the virtue of being in the right place at the right time
* Brute force approaches to distribution
* Bloat to accommodate everyone

## What taste offers...

- Opportunities to approach with a unique and differentiated angle
- Space for more than one approach to shine for the same problem
- Not all approaches may work out, but some will see the problem differently
- UI/UX -- the overall product experience -- oozing freshness
- Leading to some stickiness in some quarters
- An opportunity to convince an audience and double down further

So how are you and your organisation positioned? Do you offer a taste, or does anything/everything go?
